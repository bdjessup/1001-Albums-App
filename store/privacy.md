# Liner privacy policy

Liner is an independent companion to 1001 Albums Generator, published by Proper Applications Studio LLC.

## Projects and listening history

When you connect a project, Liner sends the project name or public summary identifier to 1001 Albums Generator to retrieve its current album, history, ratings and reviews. Liner also requests global album statistics from the generator. Artwork loads from the image addresses returned by the service. These services receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address.

Liner stores followed project identifiers, settings, cached responses and artwork on your device. Its Home Screen widget uses a local copy of the selected project's album, artist and most recent rating. Anyone who can see your widget can see that information.

Proper Applications Studio does not operate a server that receives your projects or listening history. Liner includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDK. It does not request contacts or location access.

## Your choices

Use Settings to disconnect the selected project. Removing the app deletes its local data. Your project and its ratings remain with 1001 Albums Generator. To change a rating or review, use your project on the generator's website.

If you choose to open a music service, a website or a sharing destination, that service handles the request under its own privacy policy. Emailing support shares your message and email address with Proper Applications Studio.

For privacy questions, email [Proper Applications support](mailto:contact@properapplications.net).
