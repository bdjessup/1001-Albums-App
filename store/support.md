# 1001 Albums Companion support

1001 Albums Companion follows 1001 Albums Generator projects on your device.

- **Connect a project.** Enter the project name at the end of its web address, paste the address, or paste its public summary link. A public summary lets you browse without the private project name.
- **Switch projects.** Open the project menu on Today. Choose Add Project to follow another one. Settings lets you remove the current project from this device.
- **Find your album.** Today shows the album assigned by the generator. Open it in your preferred music service. Save ratings and reviews on the generator's website.
- **Use the widget.** Add Next Listen from your Home Screen's widget gallery. Open 1001 Albums Companion to refresh the album after the generator assigns a new one.

For help, email [Proper Applications support](mailto:contact@properapplications.net). Include your device and app version. A description or a public summary link is enough. Keep your private project address out of support messages.
